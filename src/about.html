The Story of

Adrian Cole

A Chartered Accountant by qualification and a leader by practice, Adrian Cole has spent two decades at the top of the finance and energy sectors — rising from a leading global accounting firm through Meridian Power, where he led eight functions as Director Business Finance, to CFO of a multinational energy joint venture.

Along the way: billion-dollar infrastructure projects, enterprise transformations, hundreds of professionals mentored, three books published — and Lighthouse Advisory built from the ground up.

The Path

Two decades, mapped

2005

Began his career at a leading global accounting firm — audit, assurance, and a secondment to an overseas office.

2007

Qualified as a Chartered Accountant (FCA) from the national accountancy institute on first attempt — a foundation built on discipline.

2009

Joined Meridian Power. Began a thirteen-year journey through one of the country's most complex energy organisations.

2012

Led Meridian Power's $500M transmission expansion with a global engineering partner and an international equipment supplier — adding 1,000 MVA of grid capacity.

2016

Promoted to Director Business Finance — heading eight functions, reporting to CFO and Private Equity management.

2022

Founded Lighthouse Advisory. Also launched Northwind Press and began publishing books on leadership and human experience.

2024

Appointed CFO for a multinational energy joint venture. Led finance, digital transformation, and governance.

Today

Author of three published titles. Founder of Lighthouse Advisory and lead strategy of Vertex Solutions, a technology firm. Building what comes next.
